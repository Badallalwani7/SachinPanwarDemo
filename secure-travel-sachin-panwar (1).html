FAQ

Have Any Doubts?

What makes Secure Travel different from other operators?+
Secure Travel, founded by Sachin Panwar, is ATOAI-registered with 9+ years of experience. All trip leaders are BMC/AMC certified with first-aid training. We provide free travel insurance up to ₹4.5 lakhs and operate with a 100+ in-house on-ground team — no third-party dependency, ever.
Can I travel solo with Secure Travel?+
Absolutely! Many of our travelers join group departures as solo adventurers and find their tribe during the journey. Our hand-picked stays and curated itineraries ensure you have both community time and personal space.
What safety measures are in place for biking trips?+
We provide new BS6 Royal Enfield Himalayan bikes with full riding gear and tools. A dedicated backup vehicle accompanies every convoy with fully equipped mechanical support. Our Bike Marshals are expert riders who know these terrains deeply.
How does the booking process work?+
Select your trip, review the package details, complete payment in the booking section, and you'll receive a confirmation email immediately. Our team will follow up within 24 hours with full trip information and next steps.
What are All Girls Trips?+
All Girls Trips are a proud Secure Travel product where women from different backgrounds come together to explore incredible places, led by an experienced female trip leader. Safety, planning, and good company — all fully taken care of.
